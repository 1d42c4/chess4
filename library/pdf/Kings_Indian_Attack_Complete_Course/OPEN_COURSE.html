King's Indian Attack

White normally combines Nf3, g3, Bg2, O-O, d3, Nbd2 and e4. This is a family of positions, not seven moves to play regardless of Black. The core route starts with 1.Nf3; separate chapters explain the useful 1.e4 routes.
