SCANDINAVIAN DEFENSE

After 1.e4 d5 Black immediately challenges the center. The main repertoire uses 2.exd5 Qxd5 3.Nc3 Qa5, followed by development, ...c6 and ...e6 when appropriate. White gains time attacking the queen; Black must spend the remaining tempi carefully.
