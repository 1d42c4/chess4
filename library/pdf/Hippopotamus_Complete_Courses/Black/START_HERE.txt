HIPPOPOTAMUS AS BLACK

The Hippo is a restrained double fianchetto: often ...b6, ...Bb7, ...g6, ...Bg7, ...d6, ...e6, ...Nd7 and ...Ne7, with ...a6 and ...h6 only when useful. Black concedes space and frequently an objective edge. Timely departures from the full formation are essential.
