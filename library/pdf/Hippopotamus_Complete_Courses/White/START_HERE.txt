HIPPOPOTAMUS AS WHITE

White uses a restrained double fianchetto, often g3, Bg2, b3, Bb2, d3, e3, Nd2 and Ne2. The extra first move does not make passive play automatically strong. This course starts with 1.g3 and teaches earlier central action whenever the full formation would concede too much.
