TOP 50 CHESS OPENING COURSES
============================

Each opening has its own folder and one 10-page visual course PDF.
The library teaches understanding before memorization: move purpose, pawn structure, plans,
tactics, deviations, recall questions, answers, and a 14-day practice loop.

Suggested study order:
1. Learn one White opening and one answer to 1.e4 and 1.d4.
2. Do not begin another course until you can score at least 4/5 on the recall page.
3. Add a new course only after playing three slow games with the previous one.

A 'top 50' list changes with database, rating, and time control. This is a practical curriculum
of high-frequency major openings and important systems, not a claim of one universal ranking.
